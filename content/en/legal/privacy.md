## 1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to anyone who downloads, opens, or uses Natra. To use the App’s features you need an account, created with email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google. Before you create an account, the App may already process the details you enter during onboarding (for example, your birth details to show your first chart summary), as described below.

---

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to provide astrology features, run the App, keep it secure, and support you.

### 2.1 Information you provide

| Category | Examples | Why we collect it |
|---|---|---|
| Account information | Email address, password (if you use email sign-up; stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), identifiers and tokens from Apple or Google | Create and secure your account, sign you in, reset passwords |
| Profile and birth data | Name, date of birth, time of birth (optional), place of birth (city, country, coordinates) | Calculate your natal chart and personal content: portrait, daily and weekly forecasts, transits |
| Onboarding answers | What you want to focus on (for example, relationships or career) and your answers to short onboarding questions | Personalize the first experience and the offers shown in the App |
| Compatibility inputs | Another person’s name, birth date, optional birth time and place | Generate compatibility reports |
| Questions (“Ask”) | The text of questions you ask and the place they are asked from | Generate answers to your questions and keep your history |
| Support messages | The content of emails you send to support and anything you choose to attach | Respond to help requests |
| Preferences | Language and similar settings | Localize content and remember your choices |

If you enter another person’s details (for example, for a compatibility report), please make sure they are comfortable with it.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

| Category | Examples | Why we collect it |
|---|---|---|
| Usage data (analytics) | Which screens you open and which actions you take in the App (for example, onboarding steps, opening a forecast, viewing an offer), session information, app version, device model, operating system, language, approximate country derived from your IP address | Understand how the App is used, find where people get stuck, and improve the product |
| Identifiers | Your account ID; an app-instance ID generated by our analytics provider; a purchase ID used by our subscription provider | Link your purchases and usage to your account and keep your data consistent across sessions |
| Diagnostics | Crash reports and related technical data (stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, an installation ID) | Find and fix bugs |
| Server logs | IP address, request time, and technical request details on our servers | Security, abuse prevention (including rate limits), and troubleshooting |
| Quotas | Counters for limited features (for example, the number of questions you have left) | Enforce free limits and purchased credits |

We do **not** use your data for advertising, and we do **not** collect your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android). We do **not** track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

### 2.3 Location information

With your permission, Natra may access your device’s **precise or approximate location while the App is in use**.

- **Purpose:** to build the chart for a question in the “Ask” feature based on where you are when you ask it, and to suggest the nearest city.
- **Control:** you can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings. Without it you can enter or search for a place manually.
- **Not used for:** advertising, background tracking, or a continuous location history. The place linked to a question is stored with that question.

### 2.4 Place search

When you search for a place (your birth place, a partner’s birth place, or the place of a question), your search text and the selected place details (city name, country, coordinates) are sent through our servers to Google Places to find the right location.

### 2.5 Information from sign-in providers

- **Sign in with Apple** — we receive an Apple user identifier and, depending on your choices, your name and/or email (including a private relay address if you use Hide My Email).
- **Google** — we receive the sign-in tokens needed to sign you in and basic profile information Google is allowed to share (typically your email, name, and a unique account ID).

We never receive your Apple or Google password.

### 2.6 Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by **Apple (App Store)** or **Google (Google Play)**. We do not receive your card or payment details. We receive information about what you bought and its status (for example, active subscription, purchased report, remaining question credits) through Apple, Google, and our subscription provider, RevenueCat.

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

1. Provide and personalize App features: natal chart and portrait, forecasts, transits, compatibility reports, and answers to your questions.
2. Create, authenticate, and manage accounts, including password reset.
3. Store your profile, reports, and readings so they are available across sessions and devices.
4. Process purchases, restore them, and manage free limits and credits.
5. Understand how the App is used and improve it (analytics).
6. Find and fix crashes and errors (diagnostics).
7. Keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
8. Respond to support requests.
9. Comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Use.

Natra provides **entertainment and self-reflection content**. We do **not** use your data to provide medical, psychological, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

---

## 4. How readings are created

To create personal content, the data needed for a specific request (for example, your birth details, a question you asked, or a partner’s birth details) is processed by:

- our application servers;
- an astrology calculation provider, which calculates charts, transits, compatibility, and question charts;
- an AI text-generation provider (currently **Google Gemini**), which writes and translates the reading text from the calculation results and checks whether a question can be answered.

These providers receive only what is needed for the request and process it on our behalf. Generated text is based on astrology calculations and may be inaccurate or incomplete; see our Terms of Use.

## 5. Where information is stored

- **On your device** — preferences and cached content (for example, your profile and recent readings) so the App works quickly and offline.
- **On our servers and with our providers** — account data, profile, portraits, forecasts, compatibility reports, questions and answers, purchase status, and related records.

Our providers may process data in the United States, the European Economic Area, or other countries, so your information may be transferred internationally. We take steps reasonably designed to protect such transfers in line with applicable law.

## 7. Data retention

- **Account, profile, reports, and readings** — while your account exists. They are deleted when you delete your account.
- **Purchase records** — as long as needed to provide purchased features and as required by tax and accounting law.
- **Analytics and crash data** — for a limited period set in our providers’ settings, after which they are deleted or aggregated.
- **Server logs and cache** — for a short period needed for security and troubleshooting.
- **Data on your device** — until you sign out, delete your account, or remove the App.
- **Support emails** — as long as reasonably needed to resolve your request.
- **Legal retention** — longer when required by law or to resolve disputes.

When you delete your account, we permanently delete account-linked data and the authentication record, subject to short technical backup windows and any legally required retention. Purchases made through Apple or Google remain in your store account.
